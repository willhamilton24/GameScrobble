I've been a Backloggd user for a while and always wanted something that combined manual logging
with automatic play-time tracking. GameScrobble auto-polls your Steam library every 15 minutes,
builds a GitHub-style heatmap, and gives you a shareable profile at /u/<your-id>.

It's live now: https://gameroom.hdynamic.app

The free tier includes the heatmap and top games; premium ($4.99/mo) adds CSV export and
full stats across all your tracked games.

Would love to hear from Backloggd users about what's useful and what's missing — especially
around surfacing backlog insights from actual play data.
